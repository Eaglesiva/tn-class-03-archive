#### I. Choose the correct answer

1. Which of these is an example for biotic factor?
   - a. Water
   - b. Goat
   - c. Air

   - a. biotic factors
   - b. abiotic factors
   - c. both biotic and abiotic factors

   - a. plants
   - b. soil
   - c. wood

   - a. Non green plants
   - b. Green plants
   - c. Dry leaves

5. Which is an example for decomposer?
   - a. Mango tree
   - b. Bacteria
   - c. Deer

6. Which of these living things would die if there were no green plants on earth?
   - a. Herbivores
   - b. Carnivores
   - c. Omnivores
   - d. Decomposers
   Options:
   - (a) a and c only
   - (b) b and d only

   - (d) a, b, c and d
