## Questions

### Printed page 26 — Exercises (Evaluation questions)

1. Circle the odd one out:
   - i) gram, kilogram, metre
   - ii) 50g, 500g, 100cm
   - iii) 1 m, 2 kg, 5 kg

2. Fill in the blanks:
   - i) 1000 grams = _______ kg
   - ii) 2 kilograms = _______ grams
   - iii) 1 kilogram = 500 grams + _______ grams

3. Write in short form:
   - i) gram = _______
   - ii) kilogram = _______

4. Find the number of bags to be used to fill the given items, if one bag can hold 100 g:

| Items | Quantity Purchased | Weight (in grams) | Number of bags required |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Pepper powder | 1 kg | 1000 g | _______ |
| Coriander powder | 2 kg | 2000 g | _______ |
| Coffee powder | 5 kg | 5000 g | _______ |
| Dal | 10 kg | 10000 g | _______ |
| Mustard | 2 kg | 2000 g | _______ |
| Cashew nuts | 500 g | 500 g | _______ |

5. Answer the following:
   - If 100 g of ice cream costs ₹ 20, how much money should Malar pay to the shopkeeper for buying 1 kg of ice cream?
