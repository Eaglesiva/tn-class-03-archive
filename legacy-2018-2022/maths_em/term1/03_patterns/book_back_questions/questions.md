## Questions

### Printed page 37 — Try this (Rotational patterns)

---

### Printed page 38 — Activity 4 (Matching patterns)

1. Match the given patterns with the next suitable tile.
2. Observe the combination of colours and shapes, and continue the sequence.

---

### Printed page 39 — Practice (Straight-line patterns)

1. Continue the straight-line patterns in the given spaces:
   - a) Upward and downward arrow pattern
   - b) Slanted line and cross pattern
   - c) Triangle and square geometric line pattern

---

### Printed page 42 — Activity 5 (Symmetrical figures)

1. Complete the symmetrical figures based on the line of symmetry and mirror reflection.

---

### Printed page 43 — Activities 6, 7, 8 (Line symmetry)

1. Draw the lines of symmetry for the given figures.
2. Circle the non-symmetrical shapes.
3. Draw the other half from the line of symmetry to make each shape symmetrical.
4. Write whether the following letters of the alphabet have a line of symmetry:
   - A: _______
   - K: _______
   - V: _______
   - Y: _______
5. Identify the English capital letters which do not have line symmetry:
   F, G, J, L, N, P, Q, R, S, Z
